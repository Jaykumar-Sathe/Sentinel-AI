Deep Learning Pipeline

Engineered for Linguistic Nuance

Designed specifically to tackle the nuances of educational forums where debates are spirited and keyword-matching systems produce false positives.

Bidirectional LSTM

Processes comments in both forward and backward directions (64 units each), allowing the neural network to capture context surrounding sensitive academic debates without flagging harmless words.

End-to-End Vectorization

The 20,000-token vocabulary is embedded directly within the Keras model artifact using a TextVectorization layer. Raw text strings go in; calibrated class scores come out with zero external pre-tokenization.

Hybrid Safety Guardrail

Combines the deep neural tensor outputs with institutional safety heuristics, ensuring immediate escalation of physical threats while protecting constructive disagreement.

Trained on Davidson et al. Dataset

Balanced Multi-Class Cross-Entropy Optimization

Trained with inverse-frequency class weights to counteract the severe class imbalance in real-world corpora (~77% offensive language vs. 5.7% hate speech).
